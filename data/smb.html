Relay Polarity

Most relay boards are active-LOW (relay energised when signal is LOW). Enable Invert only if your board is active-HIGH.

Manual Override

Force the relay on or off regardless of weight. Works with a paired StopMyBru module or an enabled HTTP webhook relay.

HTTP Webhook Relay

Use a local HTTP relay such as Tasmota or Shelly as a StopMyBru target. The ON webhook fires when the relay/timer arms. The OFF webhook fires on manual stop or when target weight is reached.
